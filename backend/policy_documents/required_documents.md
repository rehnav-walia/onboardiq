# Required Identity Documents

## Accepted Document Types

To open an account with us, you must upload one of the following identity
documents: a valid passport, a national ID card, or a driving licence. The
document must be current and not expired. We do not accept photocopies of
documents, screenshots of other apps, or documents in a language other than
English unless accompanied by a certified translation.

## Document Quality Requirements

Photos must be clear, well-lit, and show all four corners of the document.
Blurry, cropped, or glare-obscured photos will be flagged for manual review
and may delay your application. Accepted file formats are JPG, PNG, and PDF,
up to 10MB in size.

## What We Extract From Your Document

Our system reads your full name, date of birth, ID number, document type,
and expiry date directly from the uploaded document image using AI, and
compares them against the details you entered on the application form. Any
differences are flagged for a human reviewer to check — the AI itself never
approves or rejects an application.

## Minimum Age Requirement

Applicants must meet the minimum age requirement to open an account. If our
system determines from your date of birth that you do not meet this
requirement, your application will be flagged for manual review rather than
rejected automatically.
