# Application Processing Timelines

## What Happens After You Submit

After you submit your application and upload your identity document, our
system automatically extracts the details from your document and compares
them against what you entered on the form. This normally happens within
seconds of your document being uploaded.

## Typical Review Time

Once automatic extraction and comparison are complete, your application
joins the reviewer queue. Most applications are reviewed by a member of our
team within one business day. A human reviewer always makes the final
decision — no application is ever approved or rejected automatically by the
AI system, regardless of how clean or clear-cut it looks.

## If a Reviewer Needs More Information

If a reviewer needs a clearer document or additional details from you, your
application status will change to "Info Requested." You will be able to see
what is needed and upload a replacement document, after which your
application returns to the review queue.

## Decision Outcomes

Once a reviewer makes a decision, your application status becomes either
"Approved" or "Rejected." You will not be able to see internal risk scores
or reviewer notes — only the final decision and any request for more
information.
