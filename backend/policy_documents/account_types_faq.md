# Account Types FAQ

## Savings Account

A savings account is designed for holding money you don't need to access
frequently. It typically earns interest on your balance, and is a good
choice if your main goal is to set money aside over time rather than spend
it day to day.

## Current Account

A current account is designed for everyday spending and frequent
transactions, such as paying bills, making card purchases, or receiving
your salary. Current accounts do not typically pay meaningful interest, but
are built for frequent, easy access to your money.

## Choosing Between Them

If you are unsure which account type suits you, consider how often you
expect to move money in and out. Frequent daily use suggests a current
account; infrequent access with a goal of accumulating savings suggests a
savings account. You can only select one account type per application.

## Switching Account Types Later

If you would like to change your account type after your account has been
opened, please contact support directly — this is not handled automatically
by the online onboarding system.
